Salt Lake Temple

The Salt Lake Temple's construction took 40 years, from 1853 to 1893, and was marked by significant challenges like the Utah War and the need for a seismic and interior renovation completed in 2024. Mormon pioneers, led by Brigham Young, founded the temple site in 1847, and it was dedicated by Church President Wilford Woodruff in 1893. The building is a symbol of faith, with construction occurring under divine guidance and fulfilling a prophecy from Isaiah
